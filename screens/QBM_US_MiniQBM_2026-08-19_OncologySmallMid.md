Cash position is solid ($775M as of Q2 2026, projected ~$600M at year-end, runway into at least H2 2028), giving genuine time to execute, but the near-term catalyst picture is thin. Analyst sentiment is Buy-leaning (9 analysts, 44% Strong Buy) with price targets $34-46, but 2026 revenue is still expected to be de minimis and the company unprofitable.

## LNTH — Lantheus Holdings, Inc.

- **Eligibility:** Pass on the ethical/thematic screens, but see decision below — this record concludes differently from the others for a structural reason unrelated to Company Quality.
- **Material finding, not previously known to this system:** On 3 August 2026, Curium and Lantheus signed a **definitive merger agreement** — Curium US will acquire all outstanding Lantheus shares for $102.50/share in cash at closing, plus non-transferable Contingent Value Rights worth up to $12.00/share on commercial milestones. Lantheus' board has unanimously approved the deal; closing is expected in H1 2027 pending shareholder and regulatory approval. **Lantheus has suspended its full-year 2026 financial guidance as a result.**
- **Why this changes the analysis:** QBM-US's entire framework — Company Quality, durable moat, long-term compounding, "think in years rather than months" — evaluates standalone businesses as ongoing concerns to be owned for years. A company that has already signed a fixed-price cash acquisition agreement is no longer that kind of decision: its near-term equity value is now anchored to deal terms and closing probability (regulatory/shareholder approval, timing risk, potential competing bids), not to the underlying business quality this methodology is built to assess. Evaluating LNTH on Company Quality and Investment Attractiveness as if it were a standalone long-term holding would be evaluating the wrong question.
- **What the underlying business evidence still shows (for the record):** Fundamentals were genuinely strong before the merger announcement — PYLARIFY (prostate-cancer PET imaging) is the majority of revenue, Q2 2026 revenue of $388.2M and EPS beat estimates, and a new formulation (PYLARIFY TruVu) was FDA-approved with Q4 2026 launch expected. Competitive pressure from Telix's Illuccix and other PSMA agents was a real, disclosed risk prior to the merger news. None of this is now the operative question for an investment decision.
- **Decision:** **Not advanced — not applicable due to pending acquisition, not a Company Quality finding.** LNTH is not a long-term ownership candidate under QBM-US's framework while a definitive cash-merger agreement is outstanding. If the investor wants exposure to the merger-arbitrage spread itself (the gap between current price and the $102.50 + CVR deal terms, weighted by closing probability and timing), that is a fundamentally different, event-driven decision that sits outside Quality Before Momentum's remit and would need to be evaluated on its own terms, not folded into this Mini QBM record.
