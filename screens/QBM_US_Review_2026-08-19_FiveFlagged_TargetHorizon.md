# QBM-US Review — Five Flagged Mini QBM Names Under the 6-18 Month Target Holding Period — 19 August 2026

Triggered by [QBM_US_HorizonReview_2026-08-19.md](QBM_US_HorizonReview_2026-08-19.md), which flagged HRMY, KRYS, INVA, LQDA and CON as candidates most likely to move if re-examined under [Investment Charter v1.1's Target Holding Period](../framework/QBM_US_Investment_Charter.md#target-holding-period). Company Quality evidence from the original 19 Aug 2026 Mini QBM ([QBM_US_MiniQBM_2026-08-19_SmallMidHealthCareRemaining.md](QBM_US_MiniQBM_2026-08-19_SmallMidHealthCareRemaining.md)) is not re-gathered here. This review specifically searched for a **dated, evidenced 6-18 month catalyst** for each — the test the original Mini QBM pass wasn't applying yet — rather than assuming the horizon change automatically favours advancement.

## HRMY — Harmony Biosciences — **moved to Advance to Full QBM**

**Near-term catalyst found:** Pitolisant GR (gastro-resistant formulation) NDA accepted by FDA with a **PDUFA target date of 1 April 2027** — roughly 8 months from this review, squarely inside the window. EPX-100 topline Phase 3 data is expected mid-2027 (~10-11 months out), also inside the window, though its own PDUFA (2028) is not.

**What changed from the original Mini QBM call:** the original "Continue Monitoring" leaned on a concentration risk framed around "competition and patent timelines" — a multi-year concern now properly discounted. With a dated regulatory catalyst (Pitolisant GR PDUFA) actually inside the 6-18 month window, on top of already-strong current fundamentals (record revenue +30% YoY, net-cash balance sheet), HRMY now clears the new bar: durable-enough quality plus a specific, dated near-term event.

**Decision:** Advance to Full QBM. **Priority:** Tier 2. **Confidence:** Medium — the regulatory catalyst is real and dated, but a PDUFA date is a binary approval/rejection event, and Full QBM should treat it as such rather than assuming approval.

## KRYS — Krystal Biotech — **moved to Advance to Full QBM**

**Near-term catalyst found:** EMERALD-1 (KB801) is a registrational study enrolling ~60 patients, expected to complete enrollment by end of 2026, with an 8-week primary endpoint implying a readout in **early 2027** (roughly 5-7 months from this review) — comfortably inside the window. Separately, inhaled KB707 (an oncology-adjacent asset for late-line NSCLC) already has reported Phase 1/2 data — 36% objective response rate as monotherapy, 31% in combination with pembrolizumab — real, current efficacy signal, not a future hope.

**What changed from the original Mini QBM call:** the original "Continue Monitoring" was driven mainly by VYJUVEK concentration risk — a durability concern that matters less when the intended hold is 6-18 months, especially with a dated registrational readout (EMERALD-1) inside the window that would, if positive, be real evidence of pipeline diversification beginning to materialise within the holding period itself.

**Decision:** Advance to Full QBM. **Priority:** Tier 2. **Confidence:** Medium — strong current execution and a dated near-term readout; the German pricing-negotiation headwind (flagged in the original Mini QBM) remains a live, near-term item Full QBM should track, not something the horizon change resolves.

## INVA — Innoviva — **moved to Advance to Full QBM**

**Near-term catalyst found:** unlike the other four, INVA's case isn't a single dated event — it's that the "2031 patent cliff" bear case (Goldman Sachs' stated reason for its Sell rating) is now clearly outside even the upper bound of the target window (roughly 5 years out vs. an 18-month horizon), while the near-term earnings drivers are real and already recurring: Q1 2026 alone saw $157.7M + $33.6M in fair-value gains from the strategic-investment portfolio, IST (specialty therapeutics) net product sales growing 40-50% annually with 26% US growth, and a stable, slightly growing GSK royalty base. A new wholly-owned asset (Nortiva Bio) launched in Q2 2026, adding a further near-term growth vector.

**What changed from the original Mini QBM call:** the original "Continue Monitoring" leaned heavily on the Goldman 2031-cliff bear case as a genuine, credible concern — it still is credible, but its relevance to a 6-18 month hold is now properly minimal. The near-term picture (recurring investment gains, fast IST growth, stable core royalty) is solid on its own terms.

**Decision:** Advance to Full QBM. **Priority:** Tier 3. **Confidence:** Medium — near-term earnings drivers are real, but the strategic-investment fair-value gains are, by nature, volatile mark-to-market items, not guaranteed recurring cash flow; Full QBM should weigh the quality of that revenue carefully rather than extrapolating Q1's gain rate forward.

## CON — Concentra Group Holdings — **moved to Advance to Full QBM**

**Near-term catalysts found:** raised FY2026 guidance (revenue $2.275B-$2.375B, EBITDA $450M-$470M), an active $100M share buyback program running through 2027, and continued acquisition-integration-driven growth — all current, ongoing, and inside the window. A CEO transition is scheduled effective **1 November 2026** — only about 2.5 months out.

**What changed from the original Mini QBM call:** the original "Continue Monitoring" cited a long-term automation/AI risk (workplace injuries declining over time, shrinking the addressable market) as the main contradicting evidence — explicitly a multi-year-plus concern, now properly discounted. Near-term fundamentals are unambiguously strong (unanimous 9/9 analyst Buy consensus, real margin expansion, active capital return).

**Decision:** Advance to Full QBM. **Priority:** Tier 2. **Confidence:** Medium — strong near-term operating momentum, but the November 2026 CEO transition is a genuine near-term execution item (inside the window, not outside it) that Full QBM should examine specifically, not treat as a formality just because it was planned in advance.

## LQDA — Liquidia Corporation — **confirmed Not advanced, reasoning updated**

**Near-term catalyst check:** the United Therapeutics patent litigation (seeking to enjoin YUTREPIA commercialisation) is still in early procedural stages as of the most recent evidence found — a motion to consolidate and stay proceedings had a response due 13 February 2026, and **no definitive ruling date has been publicly announced**. Patent litigation of this kind commonly runs multiple years from filing (the underlying suit was filed May 2025).

**Why this doesn't change the decision:** the horizon review flagged LQDA as a candidate where "binary litigation outcome" might now read as a near-term catalyst rather than a disqualifying factor. That reframing only helps if the binary event is actually evidenced to resolve within 6-18 months — it isn't. Per the Charter's evidence philosophy, QBM-US does not invent a resolution timeline that isn't supported by evidence. Without a dated near-term resolution, LQDA's litigation overhang could easily extend well past the target holding window, which means it doesn't function as a genuine 6-18 month catalyst.

**Decision:** Not advanced (unchanged). **Reasoning updated:** not "binary litigation is inherently disqualifying" (the original framing) but "no evidence the litigation resolves within the target holding window, so it cannot be relied on as a near-term catalyst, and the underlying commercial risk (YUTREPIA could be enjoined) remains open-ended." This is a materially more precise reason than the original, and it happens to support the same conclusion — a useful check that this review process isn't simply inflating decisions upward under the new framework.
