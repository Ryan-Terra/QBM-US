## LLY — Eli Lilly

### Current price and valuation
$1,183.16 (18 Aug 2026), within a 52-week range of $685.15-$1,249.45. Analyst consensus: 23 Buy / 2 Sell, average 12-month price target $1,310.90 (other sources show individual targets from Goldman $1,283 to as high as $1,365) — roughly **11% implied upside** on the average target, more on the higher individual targets. Stock is reported down for 2026 to date despite continued fundamental execution, meaning some of the "priced near the top of its range" concern flagged at the 14 Aug Full QBM has already partly unwound via price decline rather than needing to be resolved going forward.

### Specific near-term (6-18 month) catalyst identified
Two concrete, dated items:
1. **Foundayo (oral GLP-1) commercial ramp** — already an active catalyst, not speculative: >20,000 patients treated, ~60% U.S. GLP-1 market share, 80% of prescriptions new-to-class. This is current execution evidence, and continued ramp through the target window is a genuine, evidenced reason to expect further re-rating, not merely a hope.
2. **Rule 702 (Daubert) expert-admissibility hearing in the GLP-1 gastroparesis MDL, scheduled September 2026** — roughly one month away, squarely inside the 6-18 month window. This hearing determines whether plaintiffs' causation experts are admissible, a genuine inflection point for how the litigation overhang is priced from here, in either direction.

### Litigation — reassessed, and materially worse than the 14 Aug record, not better
This is the most important new finding from this review. Evidence gathered 19 Aug 2026 shows the litigation picture has **grown**, not stabilised, since 14 Aug:
- Gastroparesis MDL 3094 case count rose from 3,763 (1 June) to 3,848 (1 July 2026) — still actively growing.
- A **new, separate MDL (No. 3163)** was formally created specifically for NAION (vision-loss) claims, with at least 86 lawsuits already pending — this is a distinct, newly-consolidated legal front beyond what the 14 Aug record captured as a single "NAION safety signal."
- A **new item not previously known to this system**: Texas Attorney General Ken Paxton filed a legal complaint alleging kickbacks and improper compensation to doctors — a state-level regulatory action distinct from the federal MDLs.
- A Class II FDA recall was issued for liraglutide injector pens (particulate matter, ~217,600 units) — an older product (not the tirzepatide/Zepbound growth driver), so limited financial materiality, but a real, disclosed quality-control item nonetheless.

### Investment Attractiveness conclusion
Genuinely mixed, and more finely balanced than the 14 Aug record suggested. The near-term catalyst case is real and specific (Foundayo ramp, September 2026 Daubert hearing), and the valuation has partly de-risked via the 2026 price decline. But the litigation overhang is demonstrably expanding on multiple fronts (case count, a new MDL, a new state AG action) rather than stabilising — this is adverse evidence relative to 14 Aug, not neutral.

### Decision
**Investment Decision: Add (unchanged).** **Implementation Readiness: Ready with Considerations (unchanged designation, considerations updated)** — the specific consideration is no longer generic "reconcile litigation case count" but now: (1) treat the September 2026 Rule 702 hearing as a defined near-term event to monitor closely, since it plausibly resolves a meaningful part of the litigation-discount question within the target holding window; (2) the Texas AG complaint is new, unverified-in-depth evidence that should be tracked, not ignored. **Decision Confidence: Medium** (down from Medium-High at the 14 Aug Full QBM) — the growing, multi-front litigation picture is a genuine negative development, even though the core GLP-1 franchise execution (Foundayo) remains excellent.

## MA — Mastercard

### Current price and valuation
$566.29 (18 Aug 2026), P/E ~31.3x, 52-week range $464.52-$601.77. Analyst consensus: Strong Buy (41 analysts), average 12-month target $665.26 — **~17% implied upside**, with some individual targets (Cantor Fitzgerald, raised 3 Aug 2026) as high as $695, and others cited up to $744.

### Specific near-term (6-18 month) catalysts identified — this is the key change from 14 Aug
The 14 Aug Full QBM record did not identify a specific near-term catalyst, which was exactly the gap flagged in the horizon review. Fresh evidence surfaces two concrete, dated items that were not fully captured before:
1. **BVNK stablecoin-infrastructure acquisition ($1.5B, plus up to $300M contingent consideration) — expected to close before end of 2026.** This is a dated integration milestone, not just a strategic direction statement, and directly extends Mastercard's crypto/stablecoin payment-rail capability across 130+ countries.
2. **The $38B Visa/Mastercard interchange swipe-fee settlement is now progressing through court, not merely "ongoing/unresolved" as characterised on 14 Aug.** A federal judge held a preliminary-approval hearing on 27 April 2026 and is actively weighing approval as of this review. Settlement-approval progress (in either direction — approval or a further contested ruling) is a genuine near-term event with real re-rating potential, materially more specific than the 14 Aug record's framing of this as an open-ended overhang.

### Investment Attractiveness conclusion
Under the 6-18 month test, MA is a meaningfully better-supported case than the horizon review initially flagged it as being. The original concern — a genuinely excellent business with no identified near-term catalyst — is addressed once the settlement's court progress and the dated BVNK closing are treated as the specific catalysts, rather than leaning only on "accelerating growth" as a generic long-term compounding story. ~17% analyst-implied upside within the target window is a real, if unremarkable, margin.

### Decision
**Investment Decision: Add (unchanged).** **Implementation Readiness: Ready (unchanged).** **Decision Confidence: Medium-High (unchanged)** — the quality case was always strong; what changed is that this review found and named the specific near-term catalysts (settlement court progress, BVNK close) the 14 Aug record lacked, rather than finding a reason to weaken the decision.
