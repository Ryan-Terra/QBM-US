## AI-exposure Eligibility check — one exclusion found during Mini QBM research

Discovery-stage screening (business-description depth) did not catch this. Company-level Mini QBM research — reading what the company's own Q2 2026 earnings call actually attributes growth to — found one name whose current, quantified revenue depends materially on the AI-infrastructure buildout, using the same test already established in the 19 Aug Tech Services Mini QBM pass (does revenue/earnings depend on selling into AI infrastructure itself, not merely "AI" as a marketing descriptor for an unrelated product):

| Ticker | Company | Why excluded |
|---|---|---|
| **NOVT** | Novanta Inc. | On the Q2 2026 earnings call, management stated Gen-AI data center exposure (lithography, advanced-node semiconductor equipment, metrology, GPU-drilling air-bearing spindles) is **17% of revenue and growing at 25%**, with that business "booked for the next two years." This is a disclosed, quantified, current, and growing revenue driver tied directly to the AI-infrastructure buildout — the same standard that excluded ATEN and ADEA in the 19 Aug pass, and materially stronger evidence (an explicit percentage) than either of those two had. |

**Two names reviewed and kept eligible, for contrast:**
- **BHE (Benchmark Electronics)** — AC&C segment +71% YoY, explicitly tied to "AI-related program wins and production ramps." This is contract-manufacturing exposure to AI-infrastructure *customers* (named program wins in one EMS segment), the same category as PLXS and other already-longlisted EMS names — not a company-level pivot to selling AI infrastructure itself. Kept eligible, exposure disclosed prominently below.
- **ROG (Rogers Corporation)** — an "AI-focused microchannel cooler" and high-frequency circuit materials are "advancing in customer validation" for AI/data-center applications — real and disclosed, but explicitly pre-revenue (not yet a quantified current driver). Kept eligible, disclosed as a forward pipeline item, not a current exclusion trigger.
