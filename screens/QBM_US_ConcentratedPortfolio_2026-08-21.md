## Why 8, not 7

Since the 19 Aug construction, 21 more companies have completed Full QBM. Screening the full standing-Add list (24 companies, 18 on the bench) for names that clear **every** hard check with **no** disclosed confidence discount — the same bar the original 6 largely don't clear post-ATH-Rule, but a real bar nonetheless — narrows to exactly three: **BDC, BHE, ROG**, all Priority Tier 2, all Add with no ownership/selling caveat. Charter's guardrail against forcing a full 8 "merely to fill slots" doesn't apply here — this isn't filling quota, it's that three genuinely qualified candidates exist for at most two remaining slots (5 to 8 is the ceiling). Expanding to 8 and selecting the strongest two of the three, rather than stopping at 7 and arbitrarily excluding a second clean candidate, uses the room the Charter actually allows.

**BDC and BHE were selected over ROG** on the strength of their catalysts, not on Company Quality alone (all three are genuinely strong businesses):
- **BDC**'s RUCKUS Networks integration and order momentum (book-to-bill 1.11) are already executing, not pending.
- **BHE**'s HPC/AI production ramp is management-dated to "late Q4 2026 into 2027" — specific and near-term.
- **ROG**'s AI-thermal product line (the microchannel cooler) is still "advancing in customer validation" — real, but pre-revenue, a weaker 6-18 month convergence case than the other two. ROG has also already re-rated +92% over the trailing year, the largest realized run of the three — a genuine Entry-Point Discipline caution even though it clears the hard ATH floor. ROG remains the strongest bench candidate and the first name considered if a ninth slot ever opens (which the Charter's 8-holding ceiling does not currently allow) or if an existing holding is replaced.
