## Advance to Full QBM (4)

### ACAD — ACADIA Pharmaceuticals Inc.
- **Company Quality:** Medium-High. Two growing, approved commercial products (DAYBUE for Rett syndrome, +30% YoY on new formulation uptake; NUPLAZID for Parkinson's psychosis, +10% YoY with the highest new-patient volume since Q1 2018) — genuine multi-product diversification, a structurally different risk profile than the single-drug names flagged elsewhere in this batch. Strong cash position ($956M) with R&D guidance lowered (efficiency gain, not a cut driven by weakness).
- **Investment Attractiveness:** Revenue +17% YoY, guidance raised to $1.24B-$1.3B. Pipeline catalyst (remlifanserin, Alzheimer's disease psychosis, Phase 2 readout Sept-Oct 2026, FDA Fast Track) adds real optionality — though the company's own "$4B peak sales potential" claim is management's estimate, not independently verified, and should be treated as such at Full QBM.
- **Strongest evidence:** Two independently growing commercial franchises reduce single-product dependency; genuine near-term binary catalyst (Alzheimer's Phase 2 data) with a credible regulatory pathway (Fast Track).
- **Contradicting evidence:** Binary trial-outcome risk on the pipeline catalyst is real; one source explicitly flagged discount/pricing pressure as an offsetting concern to raised guidance.
- **Decision:** Advance to Full QBM. **Priority:** Tier 2. **Confidence:** Medium-High.

### LGND — Ligand Pharmaceuticals Incorporated
- **Company Quality:** Medium-High. Royalty-aggregator business model, now materially more diversified after the XOMA Royalty acquisition (120+ commercial/clinical/preclinical assets added, royalty duration extended beyond a decade in many cases) — the same structural strength (diversified royalty income, not single-product dependency) that supported advancing XNCR in the oncology batch. Revenue +34% YoY, adjusted EPS +48%.
- **Investment Attractiveness:** 2026 guidance raised; Strong Buy analyst consensus with wide-ranging but consistently bullish targets ($245-$387). Counterpoint: one source's GF Value model flags the stock as 56.4% overvalued — a real, quantified valuation tension Full QBM needs to weigh, not wave past.
- **Strongest evidence:** Genuine, recent, accretive diversification event (XOMA) reducing concentration risk; broad-based royalty growth (Filspari +96%, Otuber +98%) rather than one product carrying the story.
- **Contradicting evidence:** Explicit, quantified overvaluation flag from at least one source; Kyprolis royalty (a top-3 contributor) declined 17% YoY, showing the portfolio isn't uniformly growing.
- **Decision:** Advance to Full QBM. **Priority:** Tier 2. **Confidence:** Medium — quality/diversification evidence is strong, but valuation richness is a real, disclosed open question.

### PTCT — PTC Therapeutics, Inc.
- **Company Quality:** Medium-High. Genuinely diversified across a new blockbuster launch (Sephience, product revenue >100% YoY, 1,647 commercial patients, management sees $2B+ potential), an established DMD franchise holding up despite generic pressure ($67M), and a royalty stream from Roche's Evrysdi ($71M) — three independent revenue sources, not one. Swung to GAAP profitability this quarter ($83.5M net income) with a credible path to full-year cash-flow breakeven.
- **Investment Attractiveness:** Guidance raised to $1.18B-$1.28B; strong cash position ($2.23B) with debt refinanced on favorable terms (0% coupon). Analyst signal genuinely mixed across sources — one shows 81% buy consensus and a $95.15 average target (implying ~26% upside), another shows a "Hold" rating with a $79 target — a real, disclosed source conflict, not resolved arbitrarily.
- **Strongest evidence:** Three-way revenue diversification (Sephience, DMD, Evrysdi royalty) plus a credible, near-term path to sustained profitability, not just one good quarter.
- **Contradicting evidence:** DMD franchise still faces structural generic/market pressure long-term; the analyst-rating source conflict itself signals genuine unresolved disagreement about valuation, not just normal range.
- **Decision:** Advance to Full QBM. **Priority:** Tier 1. **Confidence:** Medium-High.

### HQY — HealthEquity, Inc.
- **Company Quality:** Medium-High. Nation's largest HSA custodian (17M+ accounts) — a genuine scale-leadership position with a real regulatory tailwind (legislative HSA-eligibility expansion could add 3-4M more eligible families, a concrete, dated catalyst rather than a vague hope). Net income +67% YoY, adjusted EPS +26%, invested-asset growth (+23%) outpacing account growth — evidence the existing member base is deepening engagement, not just growing headcount.
- **Investment Attractiveness:** Strong Buy consensus (15 analysts), price target implying further upside. Custodial-yield revenue carries genuine interest-rate sensitivity — a real, disclosed macro linkage the company doesn't fully control.
- **Strongest evidence:** Scale leadership in a structurally growing category (HSA), reinforced by an actual near-term legislative tailwind, not speculation; profitability and invested-asset growth both accelerating together.
- **Contradicting evidence:** New-account growth deceleration was explicitly flagged in the same results as a genuine soft spot; revenue has real sensitivity to future interest-rate moves, a factor outside management's control.
- **Decision:** Advance to Full QBM. **Priority:** Tier 2. **Confidence:** Medium-High.
