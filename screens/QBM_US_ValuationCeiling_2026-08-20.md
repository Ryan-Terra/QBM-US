## Data quality notes

1. **CTS — genuine data gap, no number forced.** MarketBeat shows an explicit "N/A" consensus target with only 2 analysts rated in the trailing 12 months; StockAnalysis.com's forecast page for CTS would not resolve (repeated 404s) despite appearing in the search index; a search aggregator surfaced conflicting single-analyst figures ($54 DCF fair value vs. $58 single-analyst target) that are not treated as a reliable consensus. This ticker cannot be scored PASS/FAIL against the Valuation Ceiling with real evidence today and should be re-tried once a working source is found, rather than defaulted to a pass.

2. **CNXN — thin coverage, source conflict, but directionally unambiguous.** MarketBeat says no formal consensus target exists (2 analysts, no numeric target stated); Yahoo Finance states $83.00. A third aggregator's "72 analysts" figure is almost certainly an error and was discarded rather than used. The $83.00 figure was used for the table because it is internally consistent with the current price and is corroborated in direction (not magnitude) by MarketBeat's Buy/Strong Buy ratings, but confidence in the exact target number is low.

3. **The task brief anticipated the small/mid-cap biotechs (ACAD, HRMY, KRYS) would likely be too thinly covered for a meaningful consensus.** That was checked directly and turned out **not** to be true today: ACAD has 21 analysts, HRMY has 10, and KRYS has 10 — all adequate coverage levels, not thin. The names that actually turned out thinly covered in this set are industrial/tech small-caps: BDC (4), NTCT (3), DLB (4), KN (4), and CTS/CNXN (1–2, the two flagged above). This is disclosed because the a priori assumption in the brief did not match what the sources actually showed.

4. **Target "as-of" dates vary by up to about three weeks across the set** (from Jul 24, 2026 for KN to Aug 19, 2026 for ACAD and BDC). Where a target's refresh date is materially older than the price date, the premium/discount calculation still uses the most recently published consensus figure available, but the staler ones (VCYT Aug 4, NEO Jul 29, DLB Jul 31, KN Jul 24) carry more risk of being out of date than the rest and are flagged per-ticker above.

5. **No ticker in this set fails the proposed Valuation Ceiling.** Every one of the 20 tickers with a computable target trades at a discount to consensus (ranging from −2.3% for LLY to −35.4% for ARLO); none trade at any premium, let alone the >20% premium that would trigger a FAIL. This is disclosed plainly as the honest output of the check, not adjusted or second-guessed — it is a function of QBM-US's existing Add discipline already screening for undervaluation, not an artifact of this new rule.
