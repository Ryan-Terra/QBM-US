# QBM-US Target Holding Period — Impact Review on Prior Scans — 19 August 2026

Triggered by [Investment Charter v1.1](../framework/QBM_US_Investment_Charter.md#target-holding-period) and [Methodology v1.5](../framework/QBM_US_Methodology.md#changelog): QBM-US now targets a 6-18 month holding period per position rather than an implicit indefinite/multi-year hold. This document reviews every prior Investment Decision and Mini QBM decision to identify which ones were reasoned under the old (unstated) long-horizon assumption and may need to change.

**What did not change:** Company Quality evidence (moat, balance sheet, execution, management) is horizon-independent — a durable business is durable regardless of how long QBM-US intends to hold it. None of that evidence needs re-gathering.

**What did change:** Investment Attractiveness now requires a specific, evidenced reason to expect convergence (re-rating, catalyst, value realisation) within roughly 6-18 months, and long-horizon risks (multi-year-plus) now carry proportionately less weight than before. Every prior Decision was reached without this test existing — this review applies it retroactively as a screen for what needs fresh Investment Attractiveness work, not as an automatic reversal of anything.

## 14 August 2026 Full QBM — LLY and MA (both Add)

### LLY — Eli Lilly — **Flagged for re-examination**

The original Full QBM record leaned partly on long-horizon durability (patent runway to 2036/2039) as supporting evidence — this is now far less relevant to a 6-18 month hold. More importantly, the original record explicitly noted valuation as "near the top of its historical range," treating this as "a valuation observation for Full QBM to weigh properly, not disqualifying." Under the new horizon, this needs to be weighed differently: a rich entry valuation leaves less room for the further re-rating a 6-18 month thesis typically needs, which makes the valuation question more material, not just a background observation.

**What still works in LLY's favour under the new lens:** the oral GLP-1 launch (Foundayo) and continued share-gain execution against Novo Nordisk are genuine near-term catalysts, not multi-year-only value. The litigation overhang is a near-term risk either way, unaffected by horizon.

**Recommendation:** re-run Investment Attractiveness for LLY specifically asking "what, concretely, would cause LLY to re-rate or realise additional value in the next 6-18 months, given the current rich valuation" — not yet done here.

### MA — Mastercard — **Flagged for re-examination, more significant than LLY**

The original record is a genuinely strong Company Quality case (durable duopoly moat, accelerating growth, proactive stablecoin strategy) but contains **no specific near/medium-term catalyst** — it is close to the textbook "wonderful business with no plausible 6-18 month convergence reason" case the new Charter language explicitly flags as a weaker fit for QBM-US now than it would be for a genuinely indefinite-horizon strategy. Steady, accelerating growth is real and positive, but it's a multi-year compounding story, not a 6-18 month re-rating story.

**Recommendation:** re-run Investment Attractiveness for MA specifically asking whether a concrete 6-18 month catalyst exists (e.g., a specific stablecoin-strategy milestone, interchange litigation resolution, a valuation re-rating case) — the current record doesn't answer that question because it wasn't being asked yet.

### HALO, AAPL (Continue Monitoring) — Not re-examined now

Lower priority since these aren't standing Investment Decisions requiring capital allocation. Worth the same lens whenever they're next revisited.

## Summary — what actually needs doing next

1. **LLY, MA (existing Add decisions):** re-run Investment Attractiveness under the new horizon test. Not done in this review — flagged only.
2. **HRMY, KRYS, INVA, LQDA, CON (Mini QBM):** candidates most likely to move if re-examined under the new lens — recommend a fresh Investment Attractiveness pass (Company Quality evidence doesn't need re-gathering).
3. **The 7 Full QBM candidates (VCYT, NEO, XNCR, ACAD, LGND, PTCT, HQY):** Full QBM hasn't started yet, so no rework needed — the new Target Holding Period requirement will simply be applied from the start. HQY and LGND specifically need Full QBM to articulate a sharper near-term catalyst than the Mini QBM record currently provides; VCYT and LLY share the same "rich valuation vs. limited near-term re-rating room" tension worth weighing explicitly.
4. **RCUS, TGTX, ADMA, and the seven other Not-advanced names:** reviewed, no change indicated — their rejections rest on near-term evidence that the horizon change doesn't affect.

Nothing in this document is itself a new Investment Decision — it identifies where fresh Investment Attractiveness work is warranted, per the Charter's evidence philosophy (state uncertainty and required follow-up explicitly, rather than either quietly carrying old conclusions forward or reversing them without doing the work).
