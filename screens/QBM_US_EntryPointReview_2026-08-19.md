## Correction (20 August 2026)

**The original XNCR entry above was wrong, and the error was mine.** On 19 Aug I recorded XNCR as "−18.3% over the trailing year" against a "$6.92–$21.26" 52-week range, and used that to call it one of only two names genuinely priced before its move. I even flagged in the same table that the current price ($22.89 at the time) sat *above* the stated 52-week high — a direct internal contradiction — and noted "range likely stale," but published the "Ready" conclusion anyway without resolving it. That was the mistake: I saw the inconsistency and didn't chase it down before it fed into a portfolio decision.

Re-verified 20 Aug 2026: XNCR trades at $21.89, within 1% of its actual 52-week high ($21.99, set on this run), **up 178.71% over the trailing year** — not down 18%. This is comparable in magnitude to NEO (+170%/yr), which was correctly identified as the most extreme already-broken-out case in the batch. XNCR should have been graded the same way from the start.

## What this changes

**8 of the 10 Adds have already had a substantial run and are trading at or near their own 52-week highs: ACAD, NEO, XNCR, HRMY, LLY, HQY, KRYS, VCYT.** This doesn't mean the quality thesis is wrong — Company Quality is unchanged — but buying any of these today is buying after the re-rating has largely happened, not before it, which is the specific thing the investor said they want to avoid. NEO and XNCR are the most extreme cases (+170% and +179% over the trailing year respectively) — momentum stories by any definition, regardless of how genuine the underlying business improvement is.

**Only 2 of the 10 are genuinely "before breakout": MA and, more marginally, PTCT.** MA is the cleanest case — roughly flat over the trailing year (though it has drifted toward the top of its own range in the days since this review — see the 20 Aug spot-check below), with two dated catalysts (BVNK close, settlement progress) still ahead of it rather than behind it. PTCT sits further back: real outperformance already banked, but still meaningfully below its own 52-week high.

**MA spot-check, 20 August 2026:** now trading at $576.15 against its $464.52-$601.77 range — about 82% up the range, "near the top" per current data, versus ~62% up the range when this review was first written. Still a defensible Ready call (nowhere near XNCR's situation, and the near-term catalysts are unresolved court/deal events, not already-priced-in momentum), but the margin has narrowed in a matter of days and is worth watching, not treated as static.
