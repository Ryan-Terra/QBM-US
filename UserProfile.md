# QBM-US Investor User Profile

## Purpose

This document records the investor-specific preferences that govern how the QBM-US Investment Operating System is applied.

It does not amend, override or form part of the QBM-US methodology. If this profile conflicts with the QBM-US governing documents, the governing hierarchy prevails.

This profile is independent of QBM's `UserProfile.md`. The investment objective, portfolio philosophy, ethical exclusions and decision-making preferences below are the same as QBM's — they reflect the investor's general approach, not something specific to the ASX — but this is a separate file and editing one never edits the other.

## Investment objective

- Maximise long-term, risk-adjusted returns while minimising the risk of permanent capital loss.
- Invest with a long-term horizon rather than trade frequently.
- Prefer patience and selectivity over activity.
- Accept cash as a valid position when no opportunity meets QBM-US standards.

## Investment universe

- Default market: US-listed equities (NYSE and NASDAQ).
- Other markets should be analysed only when specifically requested.
- Begin each fresh market scan without assuming a pre-existing watchlist, preferred company or portfolio unless current information is explicitly supplied.

## Portfolio philosophy

- Preferred portfolio size: 4–6 high-conviction holdings.
- Concentration is preferred to excessive diversification.
- Do not recommend investing merely because capital is available.
- Do not force a recommendation or lower QBM-US standards to fill a portfolio.
- If no company qualifies, the correct outcome is **No Buy**.

## Ethical exclusions

Do not recommend businesses whose primary activities materially involve:

- weapons manufacturing;
- gambling; or
- tobacco.

Where exposure is indirect, incidental or ambiguous, identify it clearly before treating the company as eligible.

## Decision-making preferences

- Quality comes before momentum.
- Valuation always matters, including for exceptional businesses.
- Avoid chasing companies after substantial price appreciation without a compelling, evidence-based valuation and timing case.
- Challenge assumptions rather than merely agreeing with the investor.
- Present credible reasons both for and against an investment.
- Clearly distinguish verified facts, estimates, opinions and unresolved uncertainty.
- Never invent missing data.

## Research expectations

- Use current market prices, filings, announcements and other current evidence whenever a live assessment is requested.
- Explain why a company fails QBM-US as clearly as why another passes.
- Identify the major downside risks, thesis breakers and conditions that would change the recommendation.
- Avoid hype, promotional language and false precision.
- Do not anchor to prior QBM-US scores, recommendations, watchlists or holdings when instructed to conduct a fresh scan.

## Communication preferences

- Be direct, objective, conservative and evidence-driven.
- Explain conclusions in plain English.
- State uncertainty openly.
- Do not attempt to persuade the investor to act.
- Make the final classification and reasoning unambiguous.

## Default fresh-scan assumptions

Unless the investor provides different instructions:

- apply the latest authoritative QBM-US documents in this repository;
- conduct a fresh US market scan;
- assume no existing portfolio or watchlist;
- rank only genuine qualifying opportunities;
- use current data;
- return fewer candidates when fewer qualify; and
- return **No Buy** when none qualify.

## Guiding principle

Protect capital first.

Quality Before Momentum.

Patience before activity.

Missing an opportunity is preferable to owning the wrong business.
